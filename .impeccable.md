## Design Context

### Users
Indian parents (pregnancy to 18), often in multigenerational homes, usually on a phone, frequently mid-moment: a meltdown at 9pm, a tense morning before school. Also expecting parents planning ahead. They are tired, a little anxious, and want the right words fast — not an article.

### Brand Personality
Warm, grounding, unhurried. A calm, wise elder in the room who has seen this before. Reassurance first, then precise, practical help. Never clinical, never preachy, never cutesy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** light, warm paper. Used at night too, so no harsh white; contrast comes from deep warm ink, not black.
- **Culture:** rooted in India, quietly — palette (turmeric, terracotta, indigo, leaf), textures, Indian names and families in illustration. No kitsch motifs, no rangoli wallpaper.
- **Imagery:** illustration-led. An ownable cut-paper style: flat organic shapes, slight paper grain, figures at eye level with each other. No stock photography.
- **Anti-references:** generic SaaS landing pages, glassmorphism, purple/blue gradients, Western stock-photo families, dashboards full of cards.

### Design Principles
1. **Get down to eye level.** The interface speaks to a parent the way Kahiye tells parents to speak to a child: close, calm, simple.
2. **The words are the hero.** "Say this" is the product; typography and space exist to make those words land.
3. **Warmth through material, not decoration.** Paper, grain, earth colours and soft shapes carry the feeling; every ornament must earn its place.
4. **One clear next step.** Each screen has a single obvious action; everything else recedes.
5. **Built for every script.** Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil and more must look as considered as English.
